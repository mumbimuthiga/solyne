Our Story

Passion for
perfect fit

Solyne Investments was founded on the belief that every person deserves clothing that fits beautifully and reflects their unique identity. We combine traditional tailoring techniques with modern design sensibility.

Expert seamstresses with over 8 years of combined tailoring experience in both local and international fashion.

Only premium-grade fabrics sourced from trusted local and international suppliers for every project.

Every garment receives meticulous attention — from initial measurements to final fitting and delivery.

We serve individuals, families, schools, corporations, and events with equal dedication and care.

How It Works

Our Simple
4-Step Process

From your first enquiry to wearing your finished garment — we make the journey seamless.

1

Consultation

2

Precise body measurements are taken to ensure an impeccable fit unique to you.

3
